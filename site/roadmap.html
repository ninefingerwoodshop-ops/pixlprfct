In development / SVG tools

Draw it. Refine it.
Make it move.

Vector drawing and editing within Pixel Perfect’s workspace. The drawing foundation is available in alpha.21; later phases identify future work.

  1. 01

    The drawing foundation

    Available in alpha.21

    Author and edit real paths in SVG Animation.

    Pen tool & cursor

    Available in alpha.21 · November 1. Pen-icon cursor, corner anchors, dragged Bézier handles, open/closed paths, and continuation from either endpoint. Enter or Finish path saves an open path that another object can follow. Undo, save/reopen, and SVG export are verified.

    Pencil & freehand smoothing

    Available in alpha.21 · November 1. Draw editable curves with 0–100% smoothing, round stroke ends, and Shift-constrained straight lines. Shared paints, one-step undo, export, reopening, cancellation, and edit permissions are verified.

    Direct Selection & anchor editing

    Core editing and split/join available in alpha.21. Drag or nudge anchors and handles; insert a curve-preserving midpoint, delete anchors, and convert corners/smooth points. Split at an anchor, open a closed path, or join endpoints within one SVG element. Joining separate objects and multi-node selection remain open.

    Everyday canvas editing

    Movement, path deletion and Add/Replace import available in alpha.21. Drag artwork or a selected group; use Shift to constrain movement. Delete a selected path from the keyboard, inspector or palette. Add SVG artwork while preserving animation, or explicitly replace it. Undo, save/reopen, and edit permissions are verified. Multi-object selection, duplication, grouping, and organization remain planned.

    Immediate strokes & stage selection

    Available in alpha.21 · November 1. See the stroke and next segment as you draw. Escape, Enter, Finish path, or Select keeps an open path. Click artwork on the stage, including near thin strokes.

    Gradient animation

    SVG Animation available in alpha.21 · November 1. Animate linear/radial fill and stroke gradients: rotate the gradient independently of the object, and keyframe stop colors, positions and opacity. Includes Undo, save/reopen and animated SVG export.

    Independent panel windows

    Design + SVG Animation available in alpha.21 · November 1. Parent panels have icons and collapse to icon strips; collapse/expand arrows show the next action. Expanded SVG drawers place the toggle beside the logo; Design places it after the document-title dropdown and keeps one logo. Collapsed docks keep the toggle at the top, below the SVG logo. Click an icon for an adjacent in-editor flyout, or drag its heading into a separate window that follows the cursor until release. Child sections stay with their parent. Every tear-out leaves icon-only Show and Dock actions with tooltips; compact sidebars retain project, import, download and save-recovery actions. Window positions and sizes restore after refresh/restart. SVG Pen tools can dock on the right; detached Elements leaves a compact icon rail. Controls share selection and Undo. Grouped docking and named presets remain open.

    A movable Pen toolbar

    Available in alpha.21 · November 1. Pen opens a movable right-side palette with working Pen/Pencil/Direct Selection, anchor actions, and synchronized fill/stroke controls. Keyboard placement, reset, resize clamping, and read-only protection are verified.

    The floating toolbar and right inspector now share stroke, fill, opacity, and weight. Base edits preserve existing animation; paths with Morph animators stay in the separate timed-node workflow. Joining separate path elements and smoothing existing paths remain planned. See the Pen workspace specification for behavior and open scope choices.

  2. 02

    Precision & construction

    Proposed next

    Turn simple shapes into finished artwork.

    Alignment & snapping

    Align and distribute SVG objects; snap to useful geometry and guides. Build on the controls already available in Design.

    Pathfinder & Shape Builder

    Unite, subtract, intersect, and exclude shapes. Discuss Boolean operations first, followed by interactive region selection.

    Clipping masks & compound paths

    Create and edit clipped artwork and shapes with holes. Preserve the result when saving, importing, and exporting.

    Depends on path editing and multiple-object selection. Imported masks already render in SVG Animation; authoring them is the proposed work.

  3. 03

    More expressive artwork

    Proposed later

    Add personality without leaving the canvas.

    Brush & existing-path smoothing

    Pencil and freehand smoothing are available in alpha.21. Smoothing existing paths, pressure, variable-width strokes, and brush presets remain planned.

    Text on a path

    Place editable text along a curve, then adjust its offset, direction, and spacing.

    Gradient tools

    Edit gradient stops and direction on the SVG canvas. Gradient animation is available in the SVG inspector in alpha.21; on-canvas gradient handles remain planned.

    Depends on stable path and paint editing, with font and export behavior agreed before implementation.

  4. 04

    Connect & deliver

    Explore

    Carry work between tools and into a finished project.

    Design ↔ SVG Animation

    Send selected artwork between workspaces with a clear account of what stays editable and how animation is preserved.

    Export choices & accessible motion

    Choose a still at the current playhead and define reduced-motion behavior. Evaluate GIF, video, and additional animation formats against actual delivery needs.

    SVG teamwork & automation

    Discuss comments, shared editing, and AI/MCP access for SVG projects. Design supports team editing; SVG currently uses an exclusive editing lease.

    These are research candidates. Formats and shared editing need compatibility decisions before any delivery commitment.

Proposed direction / Design & shared quality

Make the details
hold up.

New tools belong alongside dependable editing. These gaps remain part of the discussion for the main Design product.

PERFORMANCE

Responsive larger files

Continue reducing canvas, property, and layout latency. The latest cross-platform measurements still exceed the 100 ms interaction target.

DESIGN WORKFLOWS

Finish the finer controls

Improve style organization, rich text, vector construction, masks, and advanced component authoring. Verify consistency, accessibility, and working controls across the workspace.

COMPATIBILITY

More faithful exchange

Improve complex design-file imports and clearly report unsupported features. Explore richer prototypes and additional export formats, including PDF.

RELIABILITY & ACCESS

Confidence in daily use

Broaden keyboard, screen-reader, sleep/reconnect, and real team-installation checks. Public Mac signing and notarization remain separate release work.
